Support

This version is being prepared for App Store review. Download availability will be shown by Apple once released.

Start with an idea

Quick sketch offers editable 30-second piano and groove ideas, plus 60-second Mandarin and English verse-and-chorus examples. Choose Studio creation for a fuller song or more developed arrangement. Your full lyrics remain editable.

Choose a song, edit the full lyrics and keep the verses, chorus, bridge and ending you want. Generate the complete song in one request. Singing, lyric clarity and structure need listening review.

See the current stage and actual elapsed time beside the creation controls. The estimated time range adapts to successful work on your device; temperature and workload can change it. On iPhone/iPad, keep the app in the foreground while the app requests an awake screen. On Mac, keep the workspace open.

Lilt Pro

Start with three free creations, each up to 30 seconds. Failed or canceled creations do not count. Keep listening to and saving existing recordings; deleting a recording does not restore a free creation.

Lilt Pro unlocks further creation, requested durations longer than 30 seconds, simple editing, sharing and export in M4A, WAV, AIFF or FLAC. The fourth successful creation and creation with a requested duration longer than 30 seconds require Pro. One $4.99 lifetime purchase in the US can be restored on supported iPhone, iPad and Mac devices with the same Apple Account. No subscription; Apple shows the local price before purchase.

Apple Support

Songs in Cantonese and other languages

Choose Cantonese after Mandarin in Singing language and supply Cantonese lyrics. Other supported singing languages are available in the same menu. Listen to the result to review pronunciation and how the lyrics are sung.

Made for your Apple devices.

The new version is in testing for iOS 26, iPadOS 26 and macOS 26 or later. Target devices are iPhone/iPad with 8 GB or more RAM and Apple Silicon Macs with 16 GB or more. Performance on the minimum-memory devices still needs validation.

Longer creation and editing require Pro. The requested duration is a target; the saved recording may differ. Time and results depend on memory, storage and device conditions. Keep the app in the foreground while creating.
