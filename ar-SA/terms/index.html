Use the app lawfully and only provide descriptions, music or other material that you are entitled to use. Creation is interpretive: exact notes, lyrics, duration, uniqueness, absence of vocals and seamless loops are not guaranteed. Listen to and review a recording before publishing it.

Applicable third-party notices are provided in the app.

Free trial and Lilt Pro

The free trial includes three successfully completed recordings of up to 30 seconds each. Failed or canceled tasks do not count; deleting a recording does not replenish the trial. Further creation, 60-second and longer recordings, continuation, editing, sharing and export require Lilt Pro. Existing recordings remain available for listening and local saving. Pro is a single non-consumable In-App Purchase, not a subscription. The US price is USD 4.99; Apple shows the local price before confirmation. A valid purchase restores on supported app versions signed into the same Apple Account. Refunded or revoked purchases no longer grant Pro access.

Continuation and versions

You can develop a longer piece by adding fresh sections while available storage and operating conditions allow. On-device editing can save a new version while retaining the original. These features do not promise unlimited duration, capacity, exact musical continuity or a particular subjective quality.

What lifetime means

Lilt Pro has no recurring payment or preset expiration date for its purchased entitlement. It does not promise an indefinite operation of Apple services, compatibility with every future operating system, cloud storage, unlimited hardware capacity or rights to every third-party work.

Apple and refunds

Apple processes purchases and applicable refund requests. The app is licensed under Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement unless a different agreement is displayed in the App Store. Your statutory consumer rights are not restricted by this page.

Availability and support

Features depend on the supported device, operating system, storage, thermal conditions, available creation resources and regional distribution. Release status is shown on this website. Contact sunkai4u@gmail.com for help. Effective date: 6 October 2026.
