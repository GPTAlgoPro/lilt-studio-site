Quyền riêng tư

Chính sách đầy đủ được cung cấp bằng tiếng Anh. Có bản tiếng Trung giản thể trong menu ngôn ngữ.

Your creations

Descriptions, musical settings and recordings for on-device creation are stored on your device. Lilt Studio does not send them to a developer-operated creation server. It does not require a separate app account, use advertising trackers or sell your personal information.

Downloads and purchases

Apple provides creation-resource downloads and processes In-App Purchases. The app checks purchase status to unlock Pro features and keeps a count of successful free creations. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple's services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing

Sharing is initiated by you. The destination app or person you choose receives the exported file and may apply its own policies. Lilt Studio has no public music feed and does not publish a recording without your action.

Support

If you email support, we receive the message, your email address and any attachments you choose to provide. Send only what is needed. Do not include private recordings or complete diagnostic logs unless requested. We use this information to address your request and do not use it for advertising.

This website

This site has no advertising or analytics scripts, tracking cookies, sign-up form or upload form. The hosting provider may process connection information such as IP address and request logs to deliver and protect the website. Site language and appearance can follow your browser and system settings.

Control and retention

You can remove recordings inside the app. Removing the app removes its local container; exported files, external backups and recipient copies must be removed separately. Apple retains purchase records under its own policies. You may contact support to request access to or deletion of information you have sent us, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Children and changes

Lilt Studio is a general-purpose creativity app and does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child sent personal information to support, contact us. Material changes to how the app handles data will be explained in an updated policy before the affected feature is used.

Contact

Lilt Studio is maintained by Kai Sun. Privacy questions: sunkai4u@gmail.com. Policy effective date: 6 October 2026.
