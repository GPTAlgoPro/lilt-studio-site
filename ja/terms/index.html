Using Lilt Studio

Use the app lawfully and only provide descriptions, music or other material that you are entitled to use. Creation is interpretive: exact notes, lyrics, duration, uniqueness, absence of vocals and seamless loops are not guaranteed. Listen to and review a recording before publishing it.

Your music and third-party rights

Lilt Studio does not claim ownership of your original input or impose a new ownership claim over your recording. Copyright protection and the ability to use an output commercially depend on applicable law and third-party rights. The app does not guarantee originality, exclusivity, copyrightability or clearance. Applicable third-party notices are provided in the app.

Free trial and Lilt Pro

The free allowance includes five successfully completed instrumentals and five successfully completed songs, counted separately, each at most 3 minutes. Failed or canceled tasks do not count; deleting recordings or reinstalling the app does not replenish the allowance. Model-resource downloads and imports do not require Pro and do not consume the allowance. Further creation after the five-work limit of a category, requested durations over 3 minutes, editing, all sharing and all audio export require Lilt Pro. Saved recordings remain available for personal listening. The initial migration from v1 to v2 provided five works in each category while preserving earlier v1 records as history. Existing v2 counts and history are retained; this update does not replenish the allowance. Pro is one non-consumable In-App Purchase, not a subscription. The US reference price is USD 4.99; Apple shows the local price before confirmation. A valid purchase restores on supported app versions with the same Apple Account. Refunded or revoked purchases no longer grant Pro access.

Creation and versions

A complete work is requested in one creation. Requested duration is a planning target and the recorded duration can differ. On-device editing can save a new version while retaining the original. Availability depends on device resources; unlimited duration, capacity and a particular subjective quality are not promised.

What lifetime means

Lilt Pro has no recurring payment or preset expiration date for its purchased entitlement. It does not promise an indefinite operation of Apple services, compatibility with every future operating system, cloud storage, unlimited hardware capacity or rights to every third-party work.

Apple and refunds

Apple processes purchases and applicable refund requests. The app is licensed under Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement unless a different agreement is displayed in the App Store. Your statutory consumer rights are not restricted by this page.

Features depend on the supported device, operating system, storage, thermal conditions, available creation resources and regional distribution. Release status is shown on this website. Contact sunkai4u@gmail.com for help. Effective date: 9 October 2026.
