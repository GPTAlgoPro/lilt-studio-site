Free trial and Lilt Pro

The free trial includes three successfully completed recordings of up to 30 seconds each. Failed or canceled tasks do not count; deleting a recording does not replenish the trial. Further creation, creation with a requested duration longer than 30 seconds, editing, sharing and export require Lilt Pro. Existing recordings remain available for listening and local saving. Pro is a single non-consumable In-App Purchase, not a subscription. The US price is USD 4.99; Apple shows the local price before confirmation. A valid purchase restores on supported app versions signed into the same Apple Account. Refunded or revoked purchases no longer grant Pro access.

Creation and versions

A complete work is requested in one creation. Requested duration is a planning target and the recorded duration can differ. On-device editing can save a new version while retaining the original. Availability depends on device resources; unlimited duration, capacity and a particular subjective quality are not promised.

Lilt Pro has no recurring payment or preset expiration date for its purchased entitlement. It does not promise an indefinite operation of Apple services, compatibility with every future operating system, cloud storage, unlimited hardware capacity or rights to every third-party work.
