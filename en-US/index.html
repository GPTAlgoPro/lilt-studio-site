Make room for your music.

Turn a mood, a scene or a few words into a musical idea. Lilt Studio gives your imagination a place to begin, with creation on your own device.

Describe an atmosphere, choose instruments and create a musical idea of around 60 seconds. Listen, then develop it further at your own pace.

02

Listen to your result, refine your idea and create another version.

03

Start with three free creations, each up to 30 seconds. Failed or canceled creations do not count. Keep listening to and saving existing recordings; deleting a recording does not restore a free creation.

04

With Lilt Pro, develop a longer piece by adding fresh sections. Continue one section at a time while available storage and device conditions allow.

05

Make simple edits to your music: trim sections, adjust volume, speed and pitch, add fades and blend sections smoothly. Preview your changes and save a new version while keeping the original. You can currently share and export WAV audio.

06

Recognize your work by covers inspired by its waveform, with a consistent color family in light and dark appearances.

Lilt Pro

Lilt Pro unlocks further creation, recordings of 60 seconds or longer, simple editing, sharing and WAV export. The fourth successful creation and creation of 60 seconds or longer require Pro. One $4.99 lifetime purchase in the US can be restored on supported iPhone, iPad and Mac devices with the same Apple Account. No subscription; Apple shows the local price before purchase.

Longer creation and editing are Pro features. New sections are limited by available storage, memory and operating conditions; processing time and results vary. Keep the app in the foreground during creation.

The new version is in testing. Public App Store release is in preparation.

The new version is in testing, for iOS 26, iPadOS 26 and macOS 26 or later. Music creation on iPhone and iPad requires at least 7 GB of memory, normally found in models with 8 GB or more; 6 GB models are not supported for creation. Processing time and results vary with the device and available resources.
