FROM IDEA TO RECORDING

Make it yours, from the first note.

01

Start with an idea

Quick sketch offers editable 30-second piano and groove ideas, plus 60-second Mandarin and English verse-and-chorus examples. Choose Studio creation for a fuller song or more developed arrangement. Your full lyrics remain editable.

02

Shape the whole song

Choose a song, edit the full lyrics and keep the verses, chorus, bridge and ending you want. Generate the complete song in one request. Singing, lyric clarity and structure need listening review.

03

Listen, then make it yours

Review your description and lyrics before creating. Optional local music planning helps prepare the work. Listen to the result, check its actual duration and reuse the idea for another version.

Songs in Cantonese and other languages

Choose Cantonese after Mandarin in Singing language and supply Cantonese lyrics. Other supported singing languages are available in the same menu. Listen to the result to review pronunciation and how the lyrics are sung. Selecting a singing language does not translate existing lyrics. Write lyrics in that language; Automatic follows the lyrics.

A local Apple workspace

Creation runs locally on the device, including the optional music planner; it does not use an external model service. The platform-specific resources are hosted by Apple and saved on the device. Target tiers are iPhone/iPad with 8 GB or more and Mac with 16 GB or more; actual device performance still needs validation.

Follow the recording’s measured waveform as you listen. Light and dark appearances keep a consistent style, with a spacious Glass workspace on iPad and Mac.

ONE PURCHASE. MORE ROOM.

Keep creating with Lilt Pro.

Lilt Pro unlocks further creations, targets longer than 3 minutes, editing, sharing and audio export. Buy once for lifetime access. Restore a valid purchase on supported iPhone, iPad and Mac apps with the same Apple Account. No subscription; Apple shows the local price before purchase.

Music editing is included with lifetime Pro. Your originals stay unchanged. Make simple edits to your music: trim sections, adjust volume, speed and pitch, add fades and blend sections smoothly. Preview your changes and save a new version while keeping the original. Choose M4A, WAV, AIFF or FLAC when sharing or exporting audio.
